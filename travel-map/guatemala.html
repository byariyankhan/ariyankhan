Guatemala’s departments

Guatemala is divided into 22 departments. Petén, the lowland rainforest in the north, is by far the largest and holds the great Maya cities, while the volcanic highlands of the west and centre, from Huehuetenango to Sacatepéquez, are home to many of the country’s Maya peoples. Guatemala department, around the capital, has the most people.

Covering Guatemala

Shuttles and buses link the main stops. Popular ones include the colonial city of Antigua in Sacatepéquez, Lake Atitlán and its villages in Sololá, the market of Chichicastenango in Quiché, Quetzaltenango, the pools of Semuc Champey in Alta Verapaz, the Caribbean town of Livingston in Izabal, and the Maya ruins of Tikal near Flores in Petén.

Questions

How many departments does Guatemala have?

Twenty-two, from Petén in the north to Escuintla on the Pacific coast.

Which department is Tikal in?

Petén, the largest department, in the northern rainforest.

Which department is Lake Atitlán in?

Sololá, in the western highlands.
