One map for every kind of trip

Each travel map is a page of its own. Count the countries you have visited on the world map, or zoom in on a continent. Fill in the US states or the national parks, or the regions of Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, the UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal, China, Japan, South Korea and Australia. Every map shows your count and percentage, lets you add a wish list in a second colour, and turns your map into an image sized for social media.

Questions

Which travel map should I start with?

The world map if you count countries, or the map of the continent or country you travel most. Every map keeps its own marks.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, nothing uploaded, and no limit on how many maps you make.

Can I download my map?

Yes. Every map makes an image for an Instagram post, a story, a square or a wide screen, with your title and count.

Will more countries be added?

Yes. More countries’ maps of their states and regions are on the way.
