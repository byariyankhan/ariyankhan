The Czech Republic’s regions

The Czech Republic, also called Czechia, is divided into 14 regions, or kraje, in place since 2000. The capital, Prague, is a region of its own, surrounded by the Central Bohemian Region, the largest, which is also governed from Prague. Most regions are named after their main city. The western regions make up the historical land of Bohemia, and the eastern ones Moravia and Czech Silesia.

Covering the Czech Republic

Trains and buses link the regions well, and Prague is the usual starting point. Popular trips include Kutná Hora in Central Bohemia, the castle town of Český Krumlov in South Bohemia, the spa towns of Karlovy Vary, beer in Plzeň, the sandstone rocks of Bohemian Switzerland in Ústí nad Labem, and Brno and the wine country of South Moravia.

Questions

How many regions does the Czech Republic have?

Fourteen, including the capital city of Prague.

Is it Czechia or the Czech Republic?

Both are correct. Czechia is the short name and the Czech Republic the full one.

Which region is Český Krumlov in?

The South Bohemian Region, whose capital is České Budějovice.
