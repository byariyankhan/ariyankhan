Chile’s regions

Chile is divided into 16 regions, from Arica y Parinacota on the Peruvian border to Magallanes at the tip of South America. The newest, Ñuble, was split from Biobío in 2018. The Santiago Metropolitan Region, around the capital, has about 40 percent of the people. Easter Island (Rapa Nui) and the Juan Fernández Islands, far out in the Pacific, belong to the Valparaíso region but are not drawn on this map; marking Valparaíso covers them.

Covering Chile

Chile is more than 4,000 kilometres long and rarely more than 350 kilometres wide, so most travellers fly between regions. In the north, San Pedro de Atacama in Antofagasta is the base for the Atacama Desert. In the middle are Santiago, the port city of Valparaíso and the wine valleys of O’Higgins and Maule. The south has the lakes and volcanoes of La Araucanía and Los Lagos, the Carretera Austral through Aysén, and Torres del Paine in Magallanes.

Questions

How many regions does Chile have?

Sixteen, since Ñuble was created in 2018.

Which region is Easter Island in?

Valparaíso. The island is far out in the Pacific, so it is not drawn on this map; marking Valparaíso covers it.

Where is Torres del Paine?

In Magallanes, in Chilean Patagonia.

Will my map be saved?

Your marks stay in this browser. Use Copy link to keep or share your map anywhere else.
