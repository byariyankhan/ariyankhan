How many countries are in South America?

South America has 12 independent countries, the fewest of any inhabited continent. Brazil alone covers almost half of the continent’s area. French Guiana, on the north coast, is not a separate country but an overseas region of France, so it is coloured with France on this map. The Falkland Islands and South Georgia are British overseas territories; they can be marked and are counted separately.

Covering South America

Many travellers follow a loop: the Andes through Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and Bolivia, then south to Chile and Argentina, and across to Uruguay and Brazil. Guyana and Suriname, in the north-east, see far fewer visitors than their neighbours and are often the last on people’s lists. Distances are large, so long-distance buses and domestic flights are part of most trips.

Questions

Which countries are included?

Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay and Venezuela.

Is French Guiana a country?

No. It is an overseas region of France, so on this map it is part of France rather than a separate place.

Can I mark the Falkland Islands?

Yes. They are a British overseas territory, so they are counted on their own line, outside the 12.
