Argentina’s provinces

Argentina is a federal country of 23 provinces and the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, the capital, which is separate from the surrounding Buenos Aires Province. Buenos Aires Province is both the largest and the most populous, while the far south, Patagonia, is made up of a few huge and thinly populated provinces such as Santa Cruz and Chubut.

Covering Argentina

Argentina stretches more than 3,500 kilometres from north to south, so most travellers fly between regions. Highlights include Iguazú Falls in Misiones, the wine country of Mendoza, the colourful canyons of Salta and Jujuy in the north-west, the glaciers and peaks of Santa Cruz in Patagonia, the wildlife of Península Valdés in Chubut, and Ushuaia in Tierra del Fuego, one of the southernmost cities in the world.

Questions

How many provinces does Argentina have?

Twenty-three provinces plus the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires: 24 in all.

Is the city of Buenos Aires part of Buenos Aires Province?

No. The city is an autonomous district of its own, and the province’s capital is La Plata.

Which is the largest province?

Buenos Aires Province, which also has the most people.
