Jordan’s governorates

Jordan is divided into 12 governorates. Most people live in the north-west, in Amman, Irbid and Zarqa, while the desert governorates of Ma’an and Mafraq cover much of the land; Ma’an is the largest. Aqaba is Jordan’s only coastline, on the Red Sea, and the shore of the Dead Sea, the lowest point on land, runs past Balqa, Madaba and Karak.

Covering Jordan

Jordan is compact enough to drive in a week or two. Popular stops include Amman and its citadel, the Roman city of Jerash, Ajloun castle, Mount Nebo and the mosaics of Madaba, the Dead Sea, Karak castle on the King’s Highway, the Dana reserve in Tafilah, the rock-cut city of Petra in Ma’an, the desert of Wadi Rum, and the reefs off Aqaba.

Questions

How many governorates does Jordan have?

Twelve, from Irbid in the north to Aqaba in the south.

Which governorate is Petra in?

Ma’an, the largest governorate, in the south.

Which governorate is Wadi Rum in?

Aqaba, in the far south.
