Travel Map privacy

Short version: the places you mark stay on your device and in links you choose to share.

Your map

  • Stored in your browser. Your marks, title and colours are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device, so your map is there when you come back.
  • Never uploaded. The page does not send your marks anywhere. The image is made in your browser.
  • Share links carry your map. When you copy a link, your marks are written into the part of the address after the #, which browsers do not send to the website. Anyone you give the link to can see your map.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for. Clear removes a map’s marks, and clearing your browser’s site data removes everything.

What the page itself uses

Like the rest of ariyankhan.com, this page uses Google Analytics to count visits in aggregate, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and carries the Google AdSense script that verifies the site. These services see the kind of information any web page request carries, such as your IP address and browser; they do not see your map. The full details are in the site’s Privacy Policy.
