Uzbekistan’s regions

Uzbekistan is divided into 12 regions, or viloyatlar, the autonomous Republic of Karakalpakstan and the capital city of Tashkent, which is separate from the Tashkent Region around it. Karakalpakstan, in the west around the shrinking Aral Sea, covers more than a third of the country. The Fergana Valley in the east, shared by the Fergana, Andijan and Namangan regions, is the most densely populated area.

Covering Uzbekistan

Fast Afrosiyob trains link Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara, which makes the Silk Road cities easy to string together. Popular stops include the Registan in Samarkand, the old city of Bukhara, the walled town of Khiva in Khorezm, Shakhrisabz in Kashkadarya, the Buddhist sites of Termez in Surkhandarya, the silk workshops of Margilan in Fergana, and the Savitsky Museum in Nukus and the ship graveyard of Moynaq in Karakalpakstan.

Questions

How many regions does Uzbekistan have?

Twelve regions, plus the Republic of Karakalpakstan and the city of Tashkent: 14 on this map.

Which region is Khiva in?

Khorezm, in the west, near the border with Turkmenistan.

Is Tashkent city part of Tashkent Region?

No. The capital is governed separately; the region around it has its centre at Nurafshon.
