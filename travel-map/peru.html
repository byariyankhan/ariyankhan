Peru’s regions

Peru is divided into 24 departments, which today are run by regional governments, plus the Constitutional Province of Callao, the port next to Lima. The city of Lima, Lima Province, has its own metropolitan government, separate from the rest of the Lima Region around it, so this map counts 26 divisions. Loreto, in the Amazon, is the largest by area, and Lima has about a third of the country’s people.

Covering Peru

Peru has desert coast, high Andes and Amazon rainforest, and most travellers fly between them. The classic route runs from Lima to Cusco, the old Inca capital, and the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu, all in the Cusco region. Arequipa has the Colca Canyon, Puno sits on Lake Titicaca, and Ica has the oasis of Huacachina and, near the coast, the Nazca Lines. Iquitos in Loreto and Puerto Maldonado in Madre de Dios are the gateways to the Amazon.

Questions

How many regions does Peru have?

Twenty-four departments and the Constitutional Province of Callao. With Lima Province, which has its own government, this map counts 26.

Which region is Machu Picchu in?

Cusco, in the south-eastern Andes.

Why are Lima Province and the Lima Region separate?

The city of Lima is governed by its own metropolitan municipality, while the rest of the department is run by the Lima regional government, based in Huacho.
