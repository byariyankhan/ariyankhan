Ecuador’s provinces

Ecuador is divided into 24 provinces across four regions: the Pacific coast, the Andean highlands, the Amazon in the east, and the Galápagos Islands. Guayas, home of Guayaquil, has the most people, and Pastaza, in the Amazon, is the largest. The Galápagos lie about 1,000 kilometres out in the Pacific, so on this map they are moved closer and shown in a box.

Covering Ecuador

Ecuador is compact, so the coast, the Andes and the Amazon can all fit into one trip. Popular stops include the old town of Quito and the Equator line in Pichincha, the Otavalo market in Imbabura, Cotopaxi volcano, the waterfalls of Baños in Tungurahua, colonial Cuenca in Azuay, and jungle lodges reached from Tena in Napo or Coca in Orellana. The Galápagos are reached by flights from Quito or Guayaquil.

Questions

How many provinces does Ecuador have?

Twenty-four, including the Galápagos Islands.

Why are the Galápagos in a box?

They lie about 1,000 kilometres off the coast, so they are moved closer to fit on the map.

Which province is Quito in?

Pichincha, in the Andean highlands.
