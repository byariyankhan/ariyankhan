Cuba’s provinces

Cuba is divided into 15 provinces and the special municipality of the Isla de la Juventud, the large island off the south-west coast. The current map dates from 2011, when the old province of La Habana was split into Artemisa and Mayabeque and the capital became the province of Havana. Camagüey is the largest province, and Havana has the most people.

Covering Cuba

Cuba is long and narrow, about 1,200 kilometres from end to end, and most travellers cross it by bus, shared taxi or hired car. Popular stops include Old Havana, the tobacco valley of Viñales in Pinar del Río, the beaches of Varadero in Matanzas, Cienfuegos and the colonial town of Trinidad in Sancti Spíritus, Santa Clara in Villa Clara, the cays off Ciego de Ávila, and Santiago de Cuba in the east.

Questions

How many provinces does Cuba have?

Fifteen, plus the special municipality of the Isla de la Juventud: 16 on this map.

Which province is Trinidad in?

Sancti Spíritus, in central Cuba.

Which province is Varadero in?

Matanzas, east of Havana.
