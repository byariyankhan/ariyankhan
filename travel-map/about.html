About Travel Map & FAQ

A simple, private way to map where you have been and share it.

What it is

Travel Map lets you colour in the US states, European countries or world countries you have visited, add the ones you want to see next, and download the result as an image. It was made by Ariyan Khan, a documentary editor who makes videos about geography, history and culture, alongside his other free tools such as Map Maker.

How the counts work

MapTotalWhat is counted
USA50The 50 states. Washington, D.C. and the territories are not on this map.

Questions

Is Travel Map free?

Yes. There is no account, no payment and no limit on how many maps you make.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The map is drawn and the image is made inside your browser. Your marks are stored in the browser and in your share link, never on a server.

Which countries does the world map count?

The 195 most widely recognised countries: the 193 United Nations members plus the Vatican and Palestine. Territories and places with disputed status can be marked and are counted separately.

Which countries does the Europe map count?

The 44 countries the United Nations places in Europe, plus Cyprus and Turkey: 46 in total. Kosovo can be marked too.

Why is my map gone?

Marks are stored in the browser, so clearing site data or using a private window removes them. A copied share link always brings your map back.

Where do the maps come from?

The US state borders come from the US Census Bureau and the country borders from Natural Earth, both public domain, prepared by the us-atlas and world-atlas projects.

Can I use the image anywhere?

Yes. The image is yours to post, print or use in a video.
