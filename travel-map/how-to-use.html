How to use Travel Map

Mark the places you have been, see your count, and turn it into an image in a minute.

1. Choose a map

Pick USA for the 50 states, Europe for its 46 countries, World for all 195 countries and many territories, or National Parks for the 63 US national parks. Each map keeps its own marks, so you can fill in all three.

2. Mark where you have been

  1. Make sure Visited is selected above the list.
  2. Click a state or country on the map, or click its name in the list. Search the list to find small places quickly.
  3. Click it again to unmark it.

The counter shows how many you have visited out of the total, and the percentage. Places outside the count, such as territories on the world map, are added on their own line.

3. Add your wish list

Switch to Want to go and click the places you plan to visit. They get a second colour on the map and in the image, so your map shows both where you have been and where you are going next. Clicking a wish-list place in Visited mode moves it to visited.

4. Make your image

  • Title: type your own, or leave it empty to use the default, such as “States I’ve visited”.
  • Colours: five themes, from light Atlas to dark Night. The map on screen changes too.
  • Image size: an Instagram post (1080 × 1350), a story or Reel (1080 × 1920), a square, or a wide 1920 × 1080 image.

Press Download image to save a PNG with your title, your count, a legend and the map.

5. Save and share

Your marks are saved in your browser automatically, so they are there when you return on the same device. Copy link copies a link that holds your whole map: open it on another device, or send it to a friend, and they see exactly what you marked.
