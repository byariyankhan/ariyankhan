Romania’s counties

Romania is divided into 41 counties, or județe, and the municipality of Bucharest, the capital, which has the same status. Timiș, in the west, is the largest county. The counties group into the historical regions of Wallachia in the south, Moldavia in the north-east, Transylvania in the centre and north-west, Banat in the west, and Dobruja on the Black Sea coast. Ilfov, the smallest county, surrounds Bucharest.

Covering Romania

Popular trips include Bran Castle and the old town of Brașov, the Saxon cities of Sibiu and Sighișoara in Mureș, and Cluj-Napoca in Transylvania; the wooden churches of Maramureș; the painted monasteries of Bucovina in Suceava; the Transfăgărășan road over the Carpathians from Argeș to Sibiu; and the Danube Delta, one of Europe’s great wetlands, in Tulcea. Constanța is the gateway to the Black Sea coast.

Questions

How many counties does Romania have?

Forty-one, plus the municipality of Bucharest: 42 on this map.

Which county is Bran Castle in?

Brașov, in Transylvania.

Where is the Danube Delta?

Mostly in Tulcea county, in the east, where the Danube meets the Black Sea.
