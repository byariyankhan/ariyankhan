How many countries are in Africa?

Africa has 54 countries that are members of the United Nations, more than any other continent, and that is the count used here. Western Sahara, whose status is disputed, and Somaliland, which declared independence but is not widely recognised, can also be marked; they are counted on their own line. Africa is the second-largest continent by area and by population, and its largest country is Algeria, while Nigeria has the most people.

Covering Africa

Most travellers explore Africa a region at a time. North Africa, with Morocco, Egypt and Tunisia, is close to Europe. East Africa is known for safaris in Kenya and Tanzania, and southern Africa links South Africa, Namibia, Botswana and Zimbabwe by road. The island nations of the Indian Ocean and the Atlantic, such as Mauritius, the Seychelles and Cabo Verde, are usually trips of their own.

Questions

Which countries are included?

The 54 African member states of the United Nations.

Can I mark Western Sahara?

Yes. It is counted on its own line, outside the 54, because its status is disputed.

Why can’t I see some island countries?

Small island countries get a dot on the map, and you can always find them with the search box.

Will my map be saved?

Your marks stay in this browser. Use Copy link to keep or share your map anywhere else.
