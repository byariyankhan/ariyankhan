Belgium’s provinces

Belgium has three regions: Dutch-speaking Flanders in the north, French-speaking Wallonia in the south, and the bilingual Brussels-Capital Region. Flanders and Wallonia are each divided into five provinces, and Brussels belongs to no province, so this map counts 11. A small German-speaking community lives in the east of Liège province. Antwerp is the most populous province, and Luxembourg, in the Ardennes, the largest and least crowded; it borders the separate country of the same name.

Covering Belgium

Belgium is small, and trains link almost every city in an hour or two. Most trips start in Brussels and add the medieval cities of Bruges in West Flanders and Ghent in East Flanders, the port and fashion city of Antwerp, and Leuven in Flemish Brabant. In Wallonia, the battlefield of Waterloo is in Walloon Brabant, and the forests, castles and caves of the Ardennes spread across Namur, Liège and Luxembourg.

Questions

How many provinces does Belgium have?

Ten, five in Flanders and five in Wallonia. Brussels is a region of its own outside the provinces, so this map counts 11.

Which province is Bruges in?

West Flanders.

Is Belgian Luxembourg the same as the country of Luxembourg?

No. The province of Luxembourg is part of Belgium, next to the independent Grand Duchy of Luxembourg.
