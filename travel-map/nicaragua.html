Nicaragua’s departments and regions

Nicaragua is divided into 15 departments and two autonomous regions on the Caribbean coast, the North and South Caribbean Coast regions, which have their own regional governments. The two regions cover about half of the country but hold a much smaller share of its people, who live mostly in the Pacific lowlands around Managua, León and Granada and the lakes of Nicaragua and Managua.

Covering Nicaragua

Popular stops include the colonial cities of Granada and León, Masaya volcano, the twin-volcano island of Ometepe in Lake Nicaragua and the surf town of San Juan del Sur, both in Rivas, Somoto Canyon in Madriz, the old fortress of El Castillo on the Río San Juan, and the Corn Islands off the South Caribbean Coast. Before you travel, check your government’s current advice.

Questions

How many departments does Nicaragua have?

Fifteen, plus the two autonomous regions of the Caribbean coast: 17 on this map.

Which department is Ometepe in?

Rivas, in the south-west. The island sits in Lake Nicaragua.

Where are the Corn Islands?

Off the Caribbean coast, in the South Caribbean Coast autonomous region.
