Costa Rica’s provinces

Costa Rica is divided into seven provinces, each named after its capital except Guanacaste, whose capital is Liberia. Four of them, San José, Alajuela, Cartago and Heredia, meet in the Central Valley, where most Costa Ricans live. Puntarenas, the largest, runs along most of the Pacific coast, and Limón covers the Caribbean side. Cocos Island, a national park far out in the Pacific, belongs to Puntarenas but is not drawn on this map.

Covering Costa Rica

Costa Rica is small, and buses and shuttles link the main parks. Popular stops include Arenal volcano and La Fortuna in Alajuela, the cloud forest of Monteverde and the Manuel Antonio and Corcovado parks in Puntarenas, the beaches of Guanacaste, Irazú volcano in Cartago, and Tortuguero and Puerto Viejo on the Caribbean coast of Limón. Most international flights land in San José or in Liberia, Guanacaste.

Questions

How many provinces does Costa Rica have?

Seven: San José, Alajuela, Cartago, Heredia, Guanacaste, Puntarenas and Limón.

Which province is Arenal in?

Alajuela. The town of La Fortuna is the base for the volcano.

Which province is Monteverde in?

Puntarenas, although the cloud forest sits high in the mountains.
