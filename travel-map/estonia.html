Estonia’s counties

Estonia is divided into 15 counties, or maakonnad. Harju county, around the capital Tallinn, has nearly half of the country’s people. The two largest islands are counties of their own: Saare county is Saaremaa and its neighbours, and Hiiu county is Hiiumaa. Ida-Viru, in the north-east, borders Russia at the city of Narva.

Covering Estonia

Estonia is small, and buses reach every county. Popular stops include the medieval old town of Tallinn, Lahemaa National Park east of the capital, the university city of Tartu, the summer resort of Pärnu, the bogs of Soomaa, Narva castle on the Russian border, and the windmills, juniper and quiet coast of Saaremaa and Hiiumaa, reached by ferry.

Questions

How many counties does Estonia have?

Fifteen, from Harju around Tallinn to Võru in the south-east.

Which county is Saaremaa?

Saare county, which covers Saaremaa, Estonia’s largest island, and the islands around it.

Which county is Tallinn in?

Harju county, on the north coast.
