Uruguay’s departments

Uruguay is divided into 19 departments. Montevideo is the smallest but has around 40 percent of the people, and Tacuarembó, in the rolling grasslands of the north, is the largest. The River Uruguay forms the western border with Argentina, the Río de la Plata the southern coast, and the Atlantic beaches of Maldonado and Rocha run east towards Brazil.

Covering Uruguay

Uruguay is compact, and good buses link every department. Popular stops include Montevideo, the colonial old town of Colonia del Sacramento, a short ferry ride from Buenos Aires, the beach resort of Punta del Este in Maldonado, the wild coast of Cabo Polonio in Rocha, the hot springs of Salto, the old meat-packing works of Fray Bentos, a World Heritage site, and the cattle ranches of the interior.

Questions

How many departments does Uruguay have?

Nineteen, from Artigas in the north to Montevideo in the south.

Which department is Punta del Este in?

Maldonado, on the Atlantic coast east of Montevideo.

Which is the largest department?

Tacuarembó, in the north. Montevideo is the smallest and the most populous.
