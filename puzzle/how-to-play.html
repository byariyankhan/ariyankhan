How to Play

The arrow puzzle, Daily Training, your streak and playing with friends · About & FAQ

1. The arrow puzzle

The goal: clear every arrow off the board. Every arrow can only slide the way it points.

  • Tap an arrow. If nothing is in its way, it flies off the board.
  • A blocked tap costs a heart. The arrow waits, and leaves by itself once its path is clear. Tapping it again costs nothing.
  • Hearts per board: four on Easy and Normal, three on Hard, two on Expert and Master. Lose them all and the board is over.
  • Plan ahead: find the arrows that are free now, and the ones that will be free once others have gone.

2. Hints and checks

  • Hint (💡): lights up an arrow that is free to go. It adds 5 seconds to your time. Three per board on Easy and Normal, two on Hard and Expert, one on Master.
  • Check: press and hold an arrow to see its path, green if it can go and red if something blocks it. Four checks per board.
  • Run out? In the app you can choose to watch a short ad for one more; on the website it is free.

3. Out of hearts

  • Get a free life carries the board on with one heart.
  • Try again starts the same board from the beginning.
  • Lose the same board twice and you are offered New layout, an easier deal of the same board. After a third loss you can Skip for now and come back to it later.
  • Leaving a board keeps your progress and hearts: it carries on where you left it.

4. Levels, stars and difficulty

  • The world tour starts from your home country and runs through all 197 countries, with animal, bird, landmark and picture boards between them. Clearing a country tells you its capital, population and region.
  • Stars: three for a board cleared without losing a heart, two for one heart lost, one otherwise.
  • Difficulty follows you, from Easy to Master: clears on the first try move you up, and a clear the Focus bar reads as Locked in moves you up a whole level of difficulty; lost boards move you down. No board comes round again once you have cleared it. Every board you clear, and every Daily Training puzzle you clear, is one level.

5. Daily Training

Four short games on real paintings, free every day. Each one shows you something to remember, then asks you to use it. A tick counts down the last three seconds before the painting comes apart or goes.

  • Restore the Canvas: the painting hangs whole for four seconds, then falls into pieces. Drag each piece from the tray to its place in the frame; drop it on another piece to swap them. Wrong tries and slow time cost points.
  • The Forgery: the original and a forged copy. Some patches of the copy are wrong: mirrored, recoloured, or taken from elsewhere in the painting. Tap them in the copy. A tap on nothing costs points, and so does slow time.
  • Gallery Memory: paintings hang in a numbered row for a few seconds, then come down shuffled. Drag each back to the number it hung at. Wrong placings cost points.
  • The Curator’s Eye: look at one painting for a few seconds. Then, several times, pick the one of four close-ups that came from it.

Scores: every round is scored out of 100, and the day’s Brain Score is the average of the rounds you played. A score of 50 or more clears the puzzle and counts as a level. A round gets harder as soon as you score 85 or more in it, and easier after a day under 50.

Hints: one hint a day is free; each hint used takes 10 points off that round. Play again deals the same painting anew, free. Play next gives a new puzzle: in the app you choose to watch a short ad for it, on the website it is free.

6. Your streak

  • A day counts when you clear a board or play a Daily Training round. Play every day to keep your streak going.
  • Streak freezes cover one missed day. You earn one by playing all four training rounds in a day, or on every 7th day of your streak, and can hold up to two. They cannot be bought.

7. Playing with friends

  • Sign in with Google to use Play with Friends. Playing on your own never needs an account.
  • Everyone at a table puts in the same stake of gold and races the same board. The first to clear it wins the biggest share of the pot, and the next to finish can win places too.
  • Gold is only a number in the game: it cannot be bought or cashed out. The weekly league ranks what you win in matches.
