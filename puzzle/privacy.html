Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · applies to the Puzzle – Train Your Brain game at ariyankhan.com/puzzle and to any Android or iOS app version of it

1. The short version

Playing on your own needs no account. The game does not ask for your name, email or contacts, your progress stays on your own device, and the only data that leaves it is the anonymous usage statistics described below. Nothing is ever sold.

Play with Friends is the one part that asks you to sign in, and signing in is your choice. If you do, a small amount of data is kept on your account so that a match knows who is who and your tour follows you to a new phone — section 2 lists exactly what, and Settings → Delete account removes all of it.

Advertising. The game can show advertising supplied by Google, which uses cookies or local storage. Section 5 explains what that means, what a rewarded advertisement is, and how to control it.

2. Playing with friends

Play with Friends asks you to sign in, because the people you play against should know it is really you and your progress should follow you to every device. Signing in is the only part of the game that needs an account: playing on your own never does.

If you sign in, our server stores three things about you: the opaque user id your provider gives us, the display name on that account, and the web address of that account's profile picture. No email address, no password, no contacts. The picture itself is never copied to us — we keep only the link, and your browser loads it straight from Google. It also keeps your gold balance and the matches you play with friends: the stake, the board and each side's time.

While you are signed in it keeps your progress as well, so that a new phone picks up your tour where you left it rather than starting again: which boards you have cleared or skipped, your stars, best times and the difficulty each was played at, your daily-board results, and the home country your tour starts from. That is the same list section 3 describes, held against your account instead of only in one browser. Settings → Delete account removes all of it — the account, the matches, the progress and every sign-in session — for good.

Your display name and profile picture are shown to the other players in a match, so that everyone can tell who is who. Nothing else about you is.

Gold is a number in the game and nothing else. It cannot be bought, sold or turned into money.

A challenge link needs no account and no server. When you send a challenge, the game builds a link that contains the board, its difficulty, your time and the name you typed. Everything sits in the link itself; nothing about the challenge is sent to us or stored anywhere by us. Whoever holds the link can play that board, so only send it to people you want to play with.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser's local storage (or the app's equivalent) so that you can continue where you left off:

  • which levels you have cleared, your stars and best times, and your daily-board results;
  • your streaks, a skill score used to adapt the difficulty, and the number of times the game has been opened;
  • your settings: sound, vibration, music, guideline dots and colour theme;
  • whether you have accepted the Terms of Service;
  • the name you type under Play with Friends, if you type one. It stays on your device and is only written into the challenge link you choose to send.

None of this identifies you. While you are signed out none of it is sent to us; while you are signed in, the progress items above are also kept on your account as section 2 describes, and the settings, streaks and accepted-terms flag are not. You can delete everything on the device by clearing the site data in your browser or uninstalling the app, and everything on the account with Settings → Delete account.

4. Analytics

Once you have accepted these terms, the web version loads Google Analytics (Google LLC) to count visits and to record when a level is completed, together with the level number, difficulty tier, time taken and stars. The Android app does not load Google Analytics. Google Analytics may set cookies and processes your IP address and device information under its own terms; we have not enabled advertising features. See Google's privacy policy. You can block analytics with a content blocker or by disabling cookies; the game works without it.

5. Advertising

The game can show advertising supplied by Google (Google AdSense and Google's H5 Games Ads). Advertising is not part of the puzzle itself: it appears only where you ask for it — the Watch an advertisement offers for an extra heart, hint or check when you have run out, and for gold on your purse. These are called rewarded advertisements: nothing starts until you press the button, and you can close one and carry on without it.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or your browser's local storage to select an advertisement, to limit how often you see the same one, to measure whether it was watched, and to detect fraud. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this game and/or other sites on the Internet. Advertisements may be personalised, which means chosen using information about your earlier browsing, or non-personalised, which means chosen from the page and your approximate location only — non-personalised advertisements still use cookies or local storage for the capping, measurement and fraud prevention above.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and out of some other vendors' cookies at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com. When advertising is switched on and you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's certified consent management platform asks for your consent before personalised advertising or advertising cookies are used, and the same message can be reopened to change or withdraw it.

What a reward is tied to. A rewarded advertisement pays out on one thing only: the network confirming the advertisement was watched through. Closing it part-way, skipping it, an advertisement that fails to load, one that something on your device blocks, or none being available at all — every one of those gives you nothing, and the game tells you which it was. There is no path that hands out a reward for an advertisement that did not run, and no advertisement is ever reported as watched when it was not.

While advertising is being set up. Google's rewarded advertising is not running in the game yet. Until it is, the same places offer a free lifeline instead: it is not an advertisement, it does not call itself one, no advertising code runs for it and no request goes to any advertising network. The day rewarded advertising is switched on, those offers become real advertisements under the rule in the paragraph above, and this policy will say so.

What the game itself sends about an advertisement is only that one finished, so the reward can be given; the count of gold advertisements you have claimed today is kept on your account, against the daily limit. No advertisement carries your name, and we receive no personal data from Google about you.

6. Fonts and hosting

The page loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google's servers (and sends your IP address to do so). The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes connection data to deliver the pages securely. Cloudflare also tells our server which country your connection comes from (a two-letter code, never your address); the game uses it once to start your World Tour from your own country, keeps that choice on your device and does not store or log the code anywhere else. You can change or switch off the home country in Settings. Level data and the world map are static files; no other request contains anything about you.

7. Permissions the game uses

  • Vibration — a short buzz when an arrow shoots or is blocked; can be switched off in Settings.
  • Audio — sound effects and music are generated on the device; no recording, no microphone.
  • Storage — for the saved progress above and for working offline (a service worker caches the game files).
  • Sharing — the Share button hands a short text to your device's share sheet; nothing is posted without you choosing where.

The game does not use your location, camera or contacts. The only account it ever uses is the optional Google sign-in described in section 2, and only if you choose it.

8. Children

Puzzle is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13 specifically. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the contact form, please let us know and it will be deleted.

9. Third parties

The third parties involved are the ones named above: Google Analytics, Google's advertising services (section 5), Google Sign-In if you use Play with Friends (section 2), Google Fonts and Cloudflare. No other third party receives data from the game. Country outlines come from the public Natural Earth dataset. Links to YouTube open YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

10. Your rights

If you have never signed in, the game holds nothing about you on our side: your data is on your device and deleting it is entirely in your hands (section 3). If you have signed in, the account data listed in section 2 is yours — you can see it in the dashboard, and Settings → Delete account erases the account, the matches, the synced progress and every session for good. One thing is kept: a one-way hash of your Google account ID (it cannot be turned back into the ID, your name or your email), along with how many advertisement rewards were claimed that day, so that signing in again with the same Google account does not collect the welcome gold a second time — a new account made later starts with 0 gold. If you would rather ask, or want a copy, use the contact form and it will be dealt with. For the data Google holds through Analytics or advertising, you can also exercise your rights with Google directly.

11. Changes

If the game ever starts collecting more than this policy describes, it will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
