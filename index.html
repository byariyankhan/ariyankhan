Editing Stages

My Editing Process

Stage 01

The Brief

I understand your vision, your audience, and your goals. A clear brief is written before a single cut is made — no guesswork.

Stage 02

Rough Cut

Building the story structure first — finding the strongest moments, setting pacing, and establishing the narrative flow.

Stage 03

Fine Cut + Graphics

Precision editing with motion graphics, captions, B-roll, lower thirds, and transitions that elevate — never distract.

Stage 04

Color + Audio

Cinematic color grading, professional audio mix, noise reduction, and music to create the perfect emotional tone.

Stage 05

Delivery + Revisions

Export in your required format, platform-optimized. 4 revision rounds included. Source files available on request.
