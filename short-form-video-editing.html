What I Do

You record. I handle the rest.

Send me your raw clips — I'll turn them into scroll-stopping short-form videos ready for every platform.

Short-form video editing depends on the first few seconds. I shape the hook, trim slow starts, and build a clear opening so Reels, TikToks, and Shorts give viewers a reason to stop scrolling.

Animated captions make short-form videos easier to watch without sound. I style word-by-word captions, highlight key phrases, and keep text readable for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

Platform-native pacing keeps short-form content moving. I remove dead air, tighten jump cuts, and create a rhythm that helps each clip feel sharp, energetic, and built for retention.

Sound design gives social media video editing more energy. I sync music, add subtle effects, clean voice audio, and time sound cues to cuts so the edit feels polished without becoming noisy.

I prepare vertical video exports for the platforms where short-form content performs best. Each 9:16 edit is framed for mobile viewing, clean captions, safe spacing, and upload-ready delivery.

Pattern interrupts stop a clip from feeling repetitive. I use zooms, visual breaks, quick overlays, and text emphasis to reset attention while keeping the short-form video focused on the message.

Problems I Solve

Sound Familiar?

These are the most common short-form struggles creators face on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. I fix all of them.

Hooks that are too slow — losing 70% of viewers in the first 3 seconds

Short-form video editing starts with the opening hook. I trim slow starts, lead with the strongest moment, and make the first seconds clear enough to stop the scroll.

No platform-native pacing — content that feels like long-form crammed into 60 seconds

Reels, Shorts, and TikToks need a different rhythm than long videos. I tighten cuts, remove dead air, and build pacing that fits mobile viewing habits.

Missing captions — losing silent viewers who scroll past without sound

Many viewers watch short-form videos muted. I add animated captions, highlight key phrases, and keep text readable for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

Boring visuals with no cuts or zooms — nothing to hold attention on screen

Short-form content needs visual movement without becoming messy. I use zooms, overlays, cuts, and pattern interrupts to keep attention on the message.

Spending hours per clip — can't post consistently because editing takes too long

Consistency matters on social platforms, but editing every clip yourself slows the whole content pipeline. I help you keep a steady publishing rhythm.

Trending audio used wrong — sound design that kills the algorithm boost

Sound design can make a short-form edit feel sharper and more native. I sync music, sound effects, and cuts so the video has energy without feeling overdone.
