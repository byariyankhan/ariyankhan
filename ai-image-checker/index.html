What this AI image checker looks for

AI generator signatures

Traces left by ChatGPT and DALL·E, Google Gemini, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, Microsoft Designer, Stable Diffusion and other AI image tools.

Content Credentials (C2PA)

The signed record of how a file was made or edited, and the name of the app that wrote it.

AI source tags and prompts

The IPTC tag that marks an image as AI-generated, and the prompts and settings Stable Diffusion tools store in PNG files.

Camera, date and location

EXIF details such as the camera or phone, when the photo was taken and its GPS coordinates.

Questions

Can this tell for sure whether an image is AI-generated?

No tool can from metadata alone. If the file says it was made with AI, that is strong evidence. If it says nothing, the metadata may simply have been removed, so it does not prove the image is real.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The file is read inside your browser and never sent to any server, so it never leaves your device.

Does it detect Google SynthID or invisible watermarks?

No. SynthID and similar watermarks are hidden in the pixels, not the metadata, and can only be read by their makers’ own tools.

Can I remove what it finds?

Yes. AI Metadata Remover strips all of it losslessly, also without uploading anything.
