What I Do

Real impact. Told with care.

Nonprofit storytelling has to move people to act without exploiting the people it's about — here's exactly what I bring to every project.

One shoot becomes several cuts — a short emotional piece for social, a longer impact story for major donors and galas, and a concise overview for grant committees.

How It Works

Simple 4-Step Process

From raw interview and program footage to a donor-ready video library — here's exactly how we work together.

Upload your interview and program footage, along with the video lengths you need and any funding deadlines.

I find the narrative arc in your footage, build the cut(s) you need, and add impact data where it strengthens the story.

You review the first cut and leave feedback. I refine tone, pacing, and framing until it's ready to share.

Receive your donor-ready video (or video library), exported and ready for your campaign, gala, or grant deadline.
