How It Works

Simple 4-Step Process

From raw recording to publish-ready, multi-platform video — here's exactly how we work together.

Upload your raw recording to a shared Drive or Dropbox folder along with notes, references, and which platforms you need it edited for.

I handle the edit: tighter cuts, clean audio, color correction, captions, and b-roll that match your brand and the platform you're publishing to.

You review the first cut and leave timestamp-based feedback. I refine pacing, captions, and visual support until it's ready to publish.

Receive publish-ready files exported for every platform you need — YouTube, LinkedIn, Shorts, or Reels — with clean, upload-ready formats.
