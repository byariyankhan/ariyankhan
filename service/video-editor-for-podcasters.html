What I Do

You talk. I cut the episode.

Send me your raw recording — I'll turn it into a polished, publish-ready episode your audience will actually watch to the end.

Long conversations wander. I trim tangents, dead air, and repeated thoughts while keeping the natural back-and-forth of the conversation intact.

I auto-punch between speakers and reframe two or more guests so whoever's talking stays properly framed, in both standard and vertical formats.

Full episode captions and audiogram-style waveform captions for clips help your content work with or without sound, on YouTube and on social feeds.

I add YouTube chapter markers and timestamps so viewers can jump straight to the topic they came for, instead of scrubbing through a long episode.

Once your full episode is locked, I can pull the best moments from the transcript and cut them into vertical clips for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.

Problems I Solve

Sound Familiar?

These are the most common issues podcast hosts face before working with a professional editor. I fix all of them.

Long, unedited episodes with rambling tangents lose YouTube viewers fast

A raw recording that works fine as audio often loses YouTube viewers who expect tighter pacing. I trim tangents and dead air without flattening the conversation.

Awkward multi-guest framing — a static wide shot that never follows who's talking

I reframe and auto-punch between speakers so the active speaker stays properly framed instead of leaving viewers staring at whoever isn't talking.

Host and remote guests sound wildly different on different mics and platforms

I level volume and clean background noise per speaker so every guest sounds consistent, no matter what mic or recording platform they used.

No clips being pulled from episodes — missing easy social reach

Once the full episode is locked, I can pull highlight moments and cut them into clips for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok so each episode reaches further.

No chapter markers — viewers can't jump to what they want

I add YouTube chapter markers and timestamps so viewers can skip to the topic they came for instead of scrubbing through a long episode.

Editing takes so long each week that publishing becomes inconsistent

Outsourcing editing on a weekly or biweekly retainer keeps your episodes going out on schedule without editing eating into your recording time.

How It Works

Simple 4-Step Process

From raw recording to a publish-ready episode — here's exactly how we work together.

Upload your raw recording — separate audio and video tracks from Riverside, Squadcast, Zoom, or an in-person multi-camera setup — along with any notes.

I edit the episode: tighter cuts, multi-guest reframing, clean audio, captions, and chapter markers matched to your show's pacing.

You review the first cut and leave timestamp-based feedback. I refine pacing, framing, and captions until the episode is ready to publish.

Receive your publish-ready full episode, exported for YouTube, plus a batch of highlight clips for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok if you need them.
