What I Do

You send footage. I handle the rest.

Working with a remote video editor doesn't mean losing control or clarity — here's exactly how the process stays simple and secure from anywhere in the world.

I use Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or Frame.io for large raw footage transfers, so your files stay organized and secure without needing to be in the same room.

Updates go out over email, WhatsApp, or Slack on your schedule, not mine — you're never waiting on a meeting to know where your edit stands.

I keep clear delivery windows and check in proactively, so a time difference never means slower communication or missed deadlines.
