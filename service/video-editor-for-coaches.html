How It Works

Simple 4-Step Process

From raw recording to a lesson your students finish — here's exactly how we work together.

Upload your raw lesson, workshop, or webinar recording to a shared Drive or Dropbox folder along with notes on structure and any chapter breaks you want.

I edit the lesson: tighter pacing, clean audio, captions, chapter structure, and b-roll or screen recording sync so it's easy for students to follow.

You review the first cut and leave timestamp-based feedback. I refine pacing, captions, and structure until the lesson is ready for students.

Receive publish-ready lessons exported for YouTube or ready to upload directly to your course platform, with clean, organized files.
