Arrow Atlas

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · applies to the Arrow Atlas game at ariyankhan.com/arrow-atlas.html and to any Android or iOS app version of it

1. The short version

Arrow Atlas does not ask you to sign up, does not collect your name, email or contacts, does not show ads and does not sell anything. Your game progress stays on your own device. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous usage statistics sent to Google Analytics, described below.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser's local storage (or the app's equivalent) so that you can continue where you left off:

  • which levels you have cleared, your stars and best times, and your daily-board results;
  • your streaks, a skill score used to adapt the difficulty, and the number of times the game has been opened;
  • your settings: sound, vibration, music, guideline dots and colour theme;
  • whether you have accepted the Terms of Service.

None of this identifies you, none of it is sent to us, and you can delete all of it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by clearing the site data in your browser (or uninstalling the app).

3. Analytics

The web version loads Google Analytics (Google LLC) to count visits and to record when a level is completed, together with the level number, difficulty tier, time taken, stars and whether the country quiz was answered correctly. Google Analytics may set cookies and processes your IP address and device information under its own terms; we have not enabled advertising features. See Google's privacy policy. You can block analytics with a content blocker or by disabling cookies; the game works without it.

4. Fonts and hosting

The page loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google's servers (and sends your IP address to do so). The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes connection data to deliver the pages securely. Level data and the world map are static files; no request contains anything about you.

5. Permissions the game uses

  • Vibration — a short buzz when an arrow shoots or is blocked; can be switched off in Settings.
  • Audio — sound effects and music are generated on the device; no recording, no microphone.
  • Storage — for the saved progress above and for working offline (a service worker caches the game files).
  • Sharing — the Share button hands a short text to your device's share sheet; nothing is posted without you choosing where.

The game does not use your location, camera, contacts or any account.

6. Children

Arrow Atlas is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13 specifically. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the contact form, please let us know and it will be deleted.

7. Third parties

Apart from Google Analytics, Google Fonts and Cloudflare named above, no third party receives data from the game. Country outlines come from the public Natural Earth dataset. Links to YouTube open YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Because the game holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over or correct; deleting your local data is entirely in your hands (section 2). If you are in the EU/UK or another place with data-protection rights and have a question about the analytics data Google holds, you can contact us or exercise your rights with Google directly.

9. Changes

If the game ever starts collecting more (for example accounts or a leaderboard), this policy will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy in Arrow Atlas: use the contact form on the homepage.

Arrow Atlas is made by Ariyan Khan. The rest of ariyankhan.com is covered by the site's own Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
