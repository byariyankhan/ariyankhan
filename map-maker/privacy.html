Privacy

Map Maker privacy

Short version: the maps you make never leave your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

What happens to your map

  • Drawing and export happen in your browser. The page downloads the border data once, then every click, colour change and export is computed on your device. PNG and SVG files are created locally and saved by your browser.
  • Share links carry the map themselves. The countries, groups and settings are encoded in the part of the link after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the web server, so this site never receives your map. Anyone you give the link to can see and edit the map, so share it only with people you mean to.
  • Copy to clipboard writes the image to your own clipboard, through your browser.
  • No account, no uploads, no storage. There is no sign-in and no server that keeps maps.

What the page itself uses

Like the rest of ariyankhan.com, this page uses Google Analytics to count visits in aggregate, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and carries the Google AdSense script that verifies the site. No advertisements are shown in the tool. These services see the kind of information any web page request carries, such as your IP address and browser; they do not see your map. The full details, including how to opt out, are in the site’s Privacy Policy.
