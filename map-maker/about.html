About Map Maker & FAQ

A free, private way to make a clean map with the right countries highlighted, in the right projection, at the right size.

What it is

Map Maker is a small browser tool for one job: a still map of the world, or part of it, with chosen countries coloured in. You pick the countries and colours, the projection and the frame, and you take away a PNG or an SVG. It is meant for the people who explain the world for a living or for fun: YouTube geography and history channels, teachers, students, journalists, newsletter writers and anyone preparing a slide.

Why it exists

I edit videos for geography, history and economics channels, and nearly every map sequence begins the same way: a base map with a few countries highlighted. The usual starting points were screenshots of web maps, Wikipedia files in the wrong colours, or a paint-bucket job in a slide program, and a clean base map could take twenty minutes a shot. Map Maker is the tool I wanted for that step: proper projections, a globe, colour groups and an export that drops straight into a timeline.

Where the map comes from

  • Borders: Natural Earth, public domain. The 1:110m set loads by default; the 1:50m set (241 countries and territories) loads when you ask for high detail.
  • Drawing: the open-source d3-geo and d3-geo-projection libraries, with topojson-client to read the border files. All of them are served from this site, not from a third party.
  • Disputed areas: drawn by who controls them today, following Natural Earth. That is a mapping convention, not a political position.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No animation. It exports stills; animation happens in your editor.
  • No cities, provinces or custom shapes, yet. Countries and territories only. Export the SVG and add the rest in a design tool.
  • No accounts and no cloud storage. Your maps live in their share links and the files you download.

Questions

Is Map Maker free, and can I use the maps commercially?

Yes to both. There is no account and no watermark, and the border data comes from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain. Maps you export are yours to use in videos (monetised or not), presentations, courses, print and social posts. No credit is required.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. Map Maker runs in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no account.

Are my maps uploaded anywhere?

No. The map is drawn and exported on your own device. A share link carries the map inside the link itself, after the #, so nothing is stored on a server. See the privacy page.

What resolution can I export?

PNG at 1×, 2× or 4× the canvas size: on 16:9 that is 1920×1080, 3840×2160 (4K) or 7680×4320 (8K). SVG is vector, so it scales to any size.

Why is a small country missing?

The default borders are Natural Earth 1:110m, which leave out micro-states such as Singapore, Malta and Bahrain. Tick “High-detail borders” to load the 1:50m set with 241 countries and territories.

How are disputed borders drawn?

They follow Natural Earth’s de facto policy: the map shows who controls an area today, not who claims it. Kashmir, Western Sahara, Taiwan, Kosovo, Crimea and similar areas are therefore drawn one particular way that some viewers will disagree with. Add a caption where it matters for your audience.

Can I make the background transparent?

Yes. Tick “Transparent ocean/background” and the PNG keeps only land and borders, plus the globe outline if it is switched on.

Can Map Maker animate a map?

No, it makes still images. To animate, bring the PNG into a video editor and move or zoom it, or animate the SVG’s shapes in After Effects. The guide explains both.
