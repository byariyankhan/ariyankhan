AI Metadata Remover privacy

Short version: your images are cleaned on your own device and are never sent to this website or anyone else.

What happens to your images

  • They are read and rewritten in your browser. The page uses the browser’s standard file access to read an image into memory, removes the metadata there and creates the clean file there.
  • There is no upload. The page has no endpoint that receives images. You can confirm it in your browser’s developer tools: cleaning a file makes no network request.
  • Nothing is stored. Files live in the tab’s memory. Clear releases them, and closing the tab removes them.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.

What the page itself uses

Like the rest of ariyankhan.com, this page uses Google Analytics to count visits in aggregate, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and carries the Google AdSense script that verifies the site. No advertisements are shown in the tool. These services see the kind of information any web page request carries, such as your IP address and browser; they never see your images. The full details, including how to opt out, are in the site’s Privacy Policy.
