What this AI metadata remover takes out

AI labels and Content Credentials

The C2PA manifest that ChatGPT, Adobe Firefly, Photoshop and Microsoft Designer attach, and the IPTC and XMP tags that mark an image as AI-generated.

Location and camera details

EXIF data such as GPS coordinates, the camera or phone model, its serial number and the time the photo was taken.

AI prompts and settings

The prompt, seed and model that Stable Diffusion tools such as Automatic1111 and ComfyUI store inside PNG files.

Everything else hidden

Editing history, comments, embedded thumbnails and data tucked after the end of the image. The picture itself is not touched.

Questions

Is it free, and are my images uploaded?

It is free, with no sign-up and no limit. Your images are cleaned inside your browser and never sent to any server, so they never leave your device.

Will it remove the “AI info” label on Instagram or Facebook?

Those labels are mostly triggered by C2PA and IPTC metadata, which this tool removes, so an image labelled only because of its metadata will normally upload without the label. Platforms can also check the pixels themselves, so no tool can guarantee it.

Does it lower the image quality?

No. JPG, PNG and WebP files are rewritten without their metadata, and the picture data is copied exactly, so the clean file looks identical and is usually smaller.

Can it remove invisible watermarks like Google SynthID?

No. SynthID and similar marks are hidden in the pixels, not in the metadata, and this tool does not try to remove them.
